# Website admin guide (for the clinic team)

Sign in at **your-website-address/admin** with your own email and password. If you received a temporary password, you will be asked to choose your own the first time (10+ characters, letters and numbers).

## Picture details (alt text, title, caption, URL)
On the **Pictures** tab click **Edit details** under any picture. You get the picture on the left and its details on the right:
- **Alt text:** describe what the picture shows (helps Google and visually impaired visitors). Applied wherever the picture appears.
- **Title:** shown when someone hovers over the picture.
- **Image caption / Description:** saved for your team (for example consent notes and source). They are not printed on the page.
- **File URL + Copy URL to clipboard:** the picture's web address.
- **Change picture:** replace it with a new photo. **Save** stores the details. **Delete from website** hides the picture on every page where it is used; nothing is erased, and the same button becomes **Restore to website**.

## Replace a picture
1. **Pictures** tab. Search by name, or filter by page.
2. Click **Replace** under the picture, choose the new photo from your computer.
3. Keep **"Crop to the same shape"** ticked. You will see the old and new side by side.
4. Click **Replace on live site**. It appears on the website within about a minute.
- Made a mistake? **History → Restore** brings back an earlier version. **Reset** puts the original back.
- Use real, recent clinic photos, clear and well lit. Very large photos are shrunk automatically.

## Add, remove or re-order photos in a gallery
**Photo galleries** tab. These are the sections with several photos (before/after pairs, patient cases, recovery progress, Norwood stages, instruments, main process).
- **+ Add item:** choose the picture(s), fill in the text (for example “Case 09”) and click Add item.
- **←  →** move an item earlier or later. **Remove** takes it off the website. Removed originals can be put back with **Restore**.
- Nothing is public until you press **Save**. **Discard** drops your unsaved changes and **Reset to original** puts the whole gallery back as it was built.
- Please only publish photos you have the patient's written permission to show.

## Add a brand-new picture anywhere
**Extra pictures** tab → **+ Add a picture**. Choose the page, where it goes (after a named section, or at the very end above the footer), the picture, a short description and an optional caption, and a size. Press **Save** to publish. You can move, edit or remove it later.

## Change what Google shows (SEO & Schema)
- **Meta title:** the blue headline in Google. Aim for 30–60 characters.
- **Meta description:** the grey text under it. Aim for 70–160 characters.
- **Schema:** hidden information that tells Google about the clinic, FAQs and so on. Only add facts that are really on the page. Leave it alone if unsure.
- Click **Save**. Use **Reset page to built-in values** to go back.

## Google Search Console, Ads, Analytics
**Tracking codes** tab: paste the snippet Google gives you into the matching box, **Save**. It is applied to every page. Only signed-in team members can see or change it.

## Team
Owners can add people, reset passwords and remove people. Everyone can see who made which change on the **Activity** tab. Change your own password under **My account**.

## Good habits
- Never share your password. Each person uses their own login.
- Sign out on shared computers.
- Tell an owner straight away when someone leaves the team so their login can be removed.
