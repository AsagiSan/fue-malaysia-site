(07)   Aftercare

After hair transplant care protocol.

Follow your doctor’s instructions. Part of your pre-operative appointment is a discussion of what to do after surgery, and your doctor will give you an overview of what to expect. Our doctors also give you written instructions customised for you.

Your scalp may look pinkish right after the procedure. Small scabs around the micro-incisions can be covered by existing hair, which keeps the transplant hidden. Most patients feel comfortable going out in public after three to seven days.

01

Pain and swelling

In the first few days, you may have mild swelling, itching and pain in both the donor and recipient areas. These usually settle within a week. For swelling, apply ice packs over the forehead and eyelids as often as possible.

02

Rest

Rest as much as possible for the first 24 hours. Keep your upper body raised at a 30–45° angle when sitting or lying down, so your head stays above your heart. This stops fluid moving down into the eyes and face.

03

Activity

Keep activity very limited for the first 48 hours: no heavy lifting, vigorous exercise or bending over at the waist. Avoid strenuous activity for one week and swimming for two weeks.

04

Bleeding

There is normally no active bleeding after the first day. If there is, press firmly on the area with a clean cloth or gauze for 10 minutes.

05

Coverings

You can wear a clean cap after the procedure, as long as it isn’t tight and doesn’t touch the grafted area. A hair replacement system should not be placed on the grafted area for at least one week. Wear a hat to keep direct sun off your scalp.

06

Scabbing

Scabs are usually gone within 7 to 10 days. Please don’t pick them, as this can pull out the follicle with the scab.

07

Camouflaging products

Products such as topical hair fibres can be used one week after the procedure, for short periods only. Shampoo them out as soon as possible.

08

Shedding and shock loss

Around 7 to 10 days after the procedure, scabs begin to fall off with shampooing. About half of female patients see mild to severe shock loss, usually from one month after the procedure, lasting three to four weeks. This phase is temporary and the shed hair grows back.

09

Regrowth

The first signs of growth appear from about four months. Substantial growth follows, and it takes a year or more to see the full result of the transplant.
