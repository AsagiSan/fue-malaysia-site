Hair loss treatment.

Hair loss treatment covers the medical and cosmetic options used to slow, stop or reverse hair loss and thinning hair (alopecia). Depending on the cause and stage, this can include medication such as minoxidil or finasteride, scalp and laser therapies, PRP (platelet-rich plasma), and, for permanent hair loss, a hair transplant. A doctor first diagnoses the cause of your hair loss and recommends the right treatment for you.

(07)   Care Guide

Before, after, and the first wash.

These are the things we ask of you around the day of your procedure. If anything is unclear, call us.

What to do in the days before your procedure.

  1. Wash your hair before the operation. Please don’t apply any external solution or hair gel.
  2. Stop using aspirin or other blood-thinning (anticoagulant) medicines one week before.
  3. Stop drinking alcohol three days before, and stop smoking one day before.
  4. If you have a heart condition, diabetes or high blood pressure, tell your doctor about the medicines you use.
  5. Bring an adjustable cap to wear after the operation.

What to do in the weeks after your procedure.

  1. Return to the clinic where the operation was done for the medical dressing and the first wash. Please don’t touch the bandaged area yourself.
  2. For one week, prefer to lie on your back, and don’t itch or rub the transplanted area. Sexual activity should also be avoided during this period.
  3. For one month, avoid public baths, pools, the sea and strenuous sport, and don’t expose your scalp to direct sunlight.

How to wash your hair after the procedure.

  1. Start washing only on the day advised by your doctor. This is usually the third day after the first wash at the clinic.
  2. Wash with warm water. Avoid water that is too hot or too cold.
  3. Use the care lotions and shampoo recommended by your doctor for the first two weeks.
  4. Don’t rub the transplanted area while lathering. Lather the shampoo lightly, then rinse.
  5. Dry with a hair dryer on a warm setting, held at a distance. Never rub with a towel; only press it lightly to take away excess water.
  6. Don’t apply any solution, hair gel or other product unless your doctor recommends it.
