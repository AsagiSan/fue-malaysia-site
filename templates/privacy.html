In short: we collect only what we need to answer your enquiry and look after you as a patient, we do not sell your data, and you can ask to see, correct or delete it at any time. A summary in Bahasa Malaysia is at the end of this page.

01Who we are

This website is operated by FUE Malaysia (“we”, “us”, “our”), a hair transplant and hair loss clinic in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. We are the “data user” for the personal data described in this policy under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia (as amended) (“PDPA”).

This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, our WhatsApp, telephone and email channels, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

02Personal data we collect

Depending on how you use the website and contact us, we may collect:

  • Enquiry details you type into our consultation form: name, email address, telephone number, Instagram handle (optional) and the treatment you are interested in.
  • Messages and conversations you send to us by WhatsApp, telephone or email, including anything you choose to tell us about your hair, scalp or general health.
  • Photographs of your scalp, hairline or donor area that you send us so that our doctors can give a preliminary assessment.
  • Technical data that your browser sends automatically, such as IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit.

You are not required to give us any of this information, but we cannot respond to an enquiry or prepare a consultation without the details needed to contact you.

03Sensitive personal data and consent

Information about your physical or mental health, including hair-loss history, medical conditions, medication, and photographs from which this can be inferred, is sensitive personal data under the PDPA. We only collect and use it with your explicit consent, or where the law otherwise allows.

By submitting the consultation form, sending us photographs or messaging us about your condition, you consent to us processing that information for the purposes in section 4. You may withdraw your consent at any time (see section 10).

04How we use your personal data

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a free consultation or appointment.
  • To give a preliminary assessment, quotation or treatment information you asked for.
  • To contact you by telephone, WhatsApp or email about your enquiry or booking, including reminders and follow-up.
  • To keep records of our communications and to meet legal, regulatory, accounting and professional obligations.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website, and to understand in general terms how it is used.

We do not sell your personal data. We will only send you marketing messages if you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

05Who we share it with

We share personal data only where necessary and only with:

  • Our doctors, clinical team and patient coordinators, who are bound by duties of confidentiality.
  • Service providers that help us run our communications and website, for example messaging (WhatsApp), email, website hosting and content-delivery providers. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Professional advisers, regulators or authorities where we are required or permitted by law to disclose information.

This website loads fonts, scripts and videos from third-party providers (for example Google Fonts, content-delivery networks and YouTube). When your browser fetches these resources the provider receives your IP address and basic browser information under its own privacy policy.

Choosing the WhatsApp button opens a chat in WhatsApp, which is provided by WhatsApp and Meta under their own terms and privacy policies.

06Transfers outside Malaysia

Some of our service providers store or process data on servers outside Malaysia. Where this happens we take reasonable steps so that your personal data receives protection comparable to that required by the PDPA, as the law requires.

07How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes above. Enquiries that do not lead to treatment are normally deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period after our last contact. Patient and medical records are kept for the period required by Malaysian healthcare law and professional guidelines.

08How we protect it

We take practical steps to protect personal data from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction, including access controls, confidentiality obligations for staff and the use of reputable providers. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send us anything you are not comfortable sharing by these channels.

09Cookies and similar technologies

This website is designed to work with a minimum of tracking. It does not use advertising cookies. Your browser or our providers may set technical cookies or use local storage that are needed to deliver the page or protect it against abuse. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may then work differently.

10Your rights

Under the PDPA you may, subject to the exceptions in the law:

  • ask for access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct personal data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • withdraw your consent to our processing, or ask us to limit it, which may affect our ability to continue to help you;
  • ask us to stop using your data for direct marketing.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 13. We may ask you to confirm your identity first and will respond within the time the law allows. We may charge a reasonable fee for access requests where the law permits.

11Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent us personal data without that consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be shown clearly on this page.
